About

I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Management, Huazhong University of Science and Technology. I received my Ph.D. in Operations and Decision Technologies from the Paul Merage School of Business, UC Irvine, where I was advised by Professor Luyi Gui.

My research uses game-theoretic and optimization models to study new business models in digital commerce and retail, with a focus on livestream commerce, the creator economy, and socially responsible operations.

In livestream commerce and the creator economy, I study how influencers and brands negotiate and share revenue; how livestreaming shapes pricing and consumer behavior; and how platforms use their control over visibility to balance organic content against sponsorship revenue and manage their creators' growth. I also study information design for livestream consultations on online medical platforms.

My livestream research also connects to my second area, socially responsible operations. The two come together in my work on rural livestream commerce, where I study who captures its gains and when competition for attention among smallholders gives way to origin fraud. I also study retail operations for food recovery, such as pricing strategies at surplus-food supermarkets.

I hold a B.S. in Applied Mathematics from UCLA and an M.S. in Interdisciplinary Statistics and Operations Research from UNC Chapel Hill.
